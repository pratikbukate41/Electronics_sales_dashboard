**Electronics Sales Dashboard – Excel**
📊 **Project Overview**

The Electronics Sales Dashboard is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze and visualize electronics sales performance. It provides a clear overview of revenue, units sold, product performance, city-wise sales, category contribution, and sales representative performance.

---

🎯 **Objectives**
Analyze overall electronics sales performance.
Track Total Revenue and Total Units Sold.
Compare sales across different cities.
Identify top-performing products.
Analyze sales performance of sales representatives.
Understand category-wise sales contribution.
Monitor monthly sales trends.

---

📌 **Dashboard Features**
Total Revenue KPI
Total Units Sold KPI
Sales by City
Sales by Product
Sales by Sales Representative
Sales by Category
Sales by Month
Month-wise Interactive Filter/Slicer

Microsoft Excel

📈 **Key Insights**

The dashboard helps identify:

Highest and lowest performing cities.
Best-selling electronics products.
Top-performing sales representatives.
Contribution of different product categories.
Monthly sales performance.
Overall revenue and unit sales.
